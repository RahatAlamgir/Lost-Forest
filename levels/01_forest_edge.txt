name: Forest Edge
theme: green
subtitle: Where every journey begins
sign: Welcome, wanderer. Use A and D to move.
sign: Press SPACE to jump. Hold it longer to jump higher.
sign: Snails hide in their shell when struck. Attack with LEFT CLICK or X, wait for them to peek out, and strike again - or stomp on them from above!
sign: Press SPACE again while in the air to DOUBLE JUMP.
sign: Too far to jump? Press RIGHT CLICK or SHIFT to DASH. You can dash once in mid-air, too!
sign: Glowing lamps are checkpoints. They also restore your health.
sign: Wooden planks can be jumped through from below. Press S to drop through them, and hold S in mid-air to drop fast.
sign: Boars charge when they spot you. Dash or jump past the charge, then strike while it is dazed. Attack twice for a combo.
sign: The door leads deeper into the forest. Stand in front of it and press W.
